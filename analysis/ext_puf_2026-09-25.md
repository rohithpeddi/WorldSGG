# PUF adapted to monocular per-timestamp WSGG: results (2026-09-25)

This is Phase 2 of `docs/EXTERNAL_BASELINES_PLAN.md`. The design and every deviation from PUF are in `setup/EXT_PUF.md`. The code is `lib/external/puf/`, `tools/ext_puf.py` and `scripts/remote/ext_puf.sh`, on branch `exp/ext-puf`.

**Protocol.**
- The front-end is W-DSGDetr++ (resnet50). We use its outputs on observed pairs only.
- Geometry comes from Pi3 in the floor-aligned frame. The prior is fitted on the AG training annotations.
- The read-out is causal and per timestamp, over all frames of the 1,511-video WorldBBox test split (48,834 frames). All arms cover 1,511/1,511 videos and 48,834/48,834 frames.
- The slots are identical to the front-end dump's: the Track-1 feature-covered ("Covered") slots.
- Scoring:
  - `evaluate_wsgg_video`, with (wc) and without (nc) constraint;
  - `tools/bucketed_breakdown.py` for the buckets (no constraint, K = 20, as in the reference tables).
- WorldWise++ and WorldWise (resnet50) are re-scored by the same scorer from their existing dumps. The scorer reproduces the reference numbers exactly:
  - W-DSGDetr++ predcls 68.5 / 38.9, OU-nt 55.3 / 26.1;
  - WorldWise++ predcls 74.8 / 54.4, OU-nt mR 48.1;
  - WorldWise++ sgdet 54.6 / 24.5, OU-nt 27.8 / 22.3.

Values are in %. "OU-nt" means OU with `not_looking_at` and `not_contacting` dropped.

**Which unobserved pairs get memory and which get the prior.** These counts are GT pairs, not triplets, over all frames. In predcls, 12,440 unobserved pairs (62%) have an earlier observation of that object and are read from memory. The other 7,472 (38%) have never been observed up to t and get only the prior. In sgdet the split is 42,187 memory to 4,924 prior-only under PUF association. Under per-class LKS it is 32,296 to 14,815: PUF's soft class evidence lets more slots map to some node.

## Answer: does PUF-style memory plus prior close the OU-nt gap to WorldWise++?

**No, particularly not on mean recall.**

- **PredCls.** PUF full raises OU-nt from the front-end's 55.3 / 26.1 to 66.1 / 34.0 R / mR@20. That closes about half of the W-DSGDetr++ → WorldWise++ gap on R (76.6) and about a third on mR (48.1). The strongest PUF variant, without the prior on observed edges, reaches 69.8 / 38.2. It is still 6.8 R and 9.9 mR behind WorldWise++, and 3.7 R / 2.7 mR behind the plain WorldWise (resnet50).
- **SGDet.** PUF full reaches OU-nt 25.2 / 10.5. Against WorldWise on the same slots (25.4 / 20.6), R is roughly equal but mR is about half. Against WorldWise++ (27.8 / 22.3, own slots) it trails on both. The no-observed-prior variant gets 28.1 / 12.7: its R matches WorldWise++, but its mR is still 9.6 lower.
- **Fusion gets its OU recall from frequent predicates.** OU R@20 for PUF reaches 78–81 in predcls, close to WorldWise++'s 82.5, but OU mR stays at 38–42 against 51.2. The class-conditional prior is by construction a frequency prior. It fills never-seen objects with each class's common predicates, which helps R far more than mR.
- **Headline metrics get worse, not better.**
  - Literal PUF full costs 2.5 wc R@20 and 4.0 wc mR@20 against its own front-end in predcls. In sgdet it costs 10.5 wc R@20: adding the prior to observed per-frame edges blurs the front-end's frame-specific evidence.
  - No PUF arm beats the W-DSGDetr++ front-end's own headline numbers, which include its learned unobserved head. The only exception is a tie at predcls nc R@50 (99.7) for the no-observed-prior variant.
- **Memory alone is weak.** Causal memory without the prior gives LKS 46.9 / 21.0 and PUF − prior 45.0 / 25.3 predcls OU-nt, both below the front-end's own learned OU head (55.3 / 26.1) on R. About 38% of unobserved pairs are objects never seen so far, and memory cannot address them.
  - PUF's uncertainty-aware association plus soft Dirichlet evidence beats naive LKS on OU-nt mR by 4.3 in predcls, from 21.0 to 25.3. It loses slightly on OU R, from 51.5 to 48.6 (a few nodes split).
  - The non-causal bidirectional LKS hold (74.2 / 31.2) shows how much "future" information the learned models' feature buffers can exploit. It is high on R and low on mR.
- **FROSS, the hard version, is clearly the worst.** Vote counting throws away the ranking information in the front-end's distributions (OO R@20 drops 92.9 → 73.6), and hard association splits nodes. "Uncertainty" (c vs b) is therefore worth +14 OU-nt R and +8 mR in predcls, and +3 R in sgdet, where (b) has the higher mR (14.3 vs 11.4).
- **Conditioning the prior on visibility (the non-PUF extension) hurts OU-nt.** It moves mass to the trivial `not_looking_at` / `not_contacting` predicates, which lowers the non-trivial recall.
- **Summary for the paper.** A strong training-free fusion with uncertainty and a co-occurrence prior recovers much of the unobserved-object recall, R more than mR, but not the mean recall. It also degrades observed-pair quality. WorldWise++'s OU-nt advantage (+6.8 R / +9.9 mR in predcls over the best PUF variant, and about +10 mR in sgdet) is therefore not explained by "memory + prior".

The table shows four patterns:
- The AG-adapted spatial factor barely matters.
- PUF's own completion threshold of 0.8 disables most completions, which is almost the same as having no prior.
- The static-scene "track" read-out lowers observed-pair wc R.
- The sgdet OO loss of the "frame" read-out ((c) OO R@20 56.9 vs 58.3 with the slot read-out, full split) comes from PUF merging low-confidence detections of different classes into one node, whose fused evidence is then shared.

## 3D boxes (side statistic, not a Phase-0 `sgdet3d` score)

Node boxes come from the accumulated Pi3 points, trimmed 5–95%, as a floor-parallel minimum-area OBB. They are compared with each slot's GT `corners_final`, matched by label (full split, PUF full):

| mode | observed slots: coverage / mean IoU / IoU≥0.15 / IoU≥0.25 | unobserved slots: coverage / mean IoU / IoU≥0.15 / IoU≥0.25 |
|---|---|---|
| predcls | 100% / 0.147 / 34.6% / 22.3% | 62.3% / 0.093 / 21.7% / 16.0% |
| sgdet | 55.8%* / 0.066 / 15.6% / 9.3% | 57.5%* / 0.038 / 8.5% / 4.7% |

\* Only slots in a pair are read out, so unpaired detections count as "no box". In sgdet the GT is the same-label annotation object.

The 3D boxes are weak: monocular Pi3 point clusters inside 2D boxes include background and occluders. This is a limit of PUF's box-to-point lifting, not of the relation read-out.

## Open issues / caveats

- These are Covered-set, Track-1 numbers. The Phase-0 Full set (all 231k slots) and the `sgdet3d` regime are not implemented yet (`tools/score_unified.py` does not exist). Re-score the dumps once it does.
- The bucketed SGDet evaluator does not enforce 2D IoU (`evaluation_recall_bucketed.py:207`), so the sgdet bucket numbers are slot/class matches.
- WorldWise++ sgdet is scored on different (dinov3) detector slots. WorldWise (resnet50) is the like-for-like sgdet comparison.
- The prior is fitted on `world4d_rel_annotations/train`; WorldBBox has no train split. `P_exist` is ~1.0 for every class in AG, so the existence gate is inert.
- The front-end for sgdet exposes no class softmax. Class evidence is the detector score on the label, spread uniformly over the rest, which drives the cross-class merges noted above.
- The UNO front-end ablation is out of scope.
- Runtime: fusion is CPU-only numpy at roughly 10–25 ms per frame per core (in PUF's 15–31 ms range); the full split takes 44–107 s wall with 12 workers. The one-off Pi3 geometry cache took about 63 min (8 workers, HDD bound). No GPU was used.
